# Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

PhotoCleaner ("we", "our", "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the PhotoCleaner iOS app processes and how it is used.

## Summary

- Your photos and videos are processed on your device. PhotoCleaner never uploads your photos.
- There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no ads.
- We do not sell your data.

## Information we process

- **Photo library:** To let you review your photos and videos, find categories such as screenshots or similar and duplicate photos, and delete the items you choose, the app reads your photo library on your device. Your photos are not uploaded to us or to any server.
- **App data stored on your device:** The app stores a small amount of data locally in its own iOS app storage:
  - the items you have marked for deletion (photo library identifiers plus basic details such as file name, dimensions and date), so your selection is still there after you close and reopen the app
  - your settings, such as the selected time range and whether you have finished the welcome screens and tutorial
  - your cleanup statistics, such as how many items you have deleted and how much space you freed
- Image thumbnails may be cached on your device to keep the app fast.

This data stays on your device. We do not have access to it.

## Permissions

- **Photo library access (read and write):** Needed to show your photos and videos for review and to delete the ones you confirm. iOS shows its own confirmation before anything is deleted. You can also choose to give access to selected photos only.
- **Add to photo library:** Used only when you choose **Save** in the share sheet to save a copy of a photo or video to your library.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

## Sharing

When you tap Share, the iOS share sheet opens. A photo or video leaves the app only if you choose a destination in the share sheet, such as Messages, Mail or another app, and it goes only where you send it. That destination's own privacy practices apply. If the photo or video is stored in iCloud Photos, iOS may first download it from your own iCloud account so it can be shared.

## Third-party services

PhotoCleaner does not include third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and it does not send your data to any server. The app uses Apple's iOS frameworks (for example, the Photos framework) to work with your library on your device.

## Data storage and retention

- App data is stored only on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it.
- Photos you delete with PhotoCleaner go to the **Recently Deleted** album in the Photos app, where iOS keeps them for up to 30 days before they are permanently removed.
- We do not require account creation to use the app.
- We do not sell personal information.

## Children's privacy

PhotoCleaner is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not collect personal information from anyone.

## Your choices

- You can unmark items before confirming deletion.
- You can remove photo access, or limit it to selected photos, in iOS Settings.
- You can uninstall the app to remove all of its local app data.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material updates will be reflected by updating the effective date above.
