Official support page for users of the PhotoCleaner iOS app. Contact us for app issues, general feedback, and feature enhancement requests.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does PhotoCleaner need access to my photos?

PhotoCleaner shows your photos and videos so you can review them and delete the ones you choose. Everything happens on your device, and PhotoCleaner never uploads your photos. If you chose **Limit Access**, the app can only show the photos you selected. You can change this in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos → PhotoCleaner**.

### I deleted something by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, for a while. Photos and videos deleted with PhotoCleaner go to the **Recently Deleted** album in the Photos app. iOS keeps them there for up to 30 days, and you can recover them from that album before they are permanently removed.

### Are my marked items saved if I close the app?

Yes. Items you mark for deletion stay marked until you delete or unmark them, even after you close and reopen the app. Open the **Review** tab to see everything that is marked. Nothing is deleted until you tap **Confirm Delete** and confirm the iOS prompt. If you delete a marked photo with another app in the meantime, it is removed from the list the next time you open PhotoCleaner.

### Why did iOS ask me to confirm the deletion?

iOS always asks for your permission before an app deletes photos or videos from your library. This is an extra safety check.
