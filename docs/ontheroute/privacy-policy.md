# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

WeatherOnTheRoute ("we", "our", "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you use the WeatherOnTheRoute iOS app and how that information is used.

## Summary

- WeatherOnTheRoute is free. There is no account, no subscription, no in-app purchase and no advertising.
- The app does not include analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across apps or websites.
- To build your route and its forecast, the app sends places and coordinates directly to the map, routing and weather services listed below. We do not run our own server and we never receive this data.
- Saved routes are stored only on your device.
- We do not sell your data.

## Information we process

- **Device location (optional):** If you allow it, the app reads your location while you are using the app, to center the map and to use your current position as a trip start. The app does not use your location in the background.
- **Trip inputs:** The places you search for, your start, stops and destination, and your departure time. These are used to build your route and the weather forecast along it.
- **Saved routes:** Routes you choose to save, and whether you have finished the welcome screens, are stored locally on your device using iOS app storage.
- **Network metadata:** Like any internet request, requests to the services below include standard technical data such as your IP address and the time of the request.

## How we use information

We use the above information to:

- Build driving routes and weather previews along your trip
- Show radar and weather alert overlays
- Save your chosen routes on your device

## Third-party services

The app contacts these providers directly. Each request contains only what that feature needs:

- **Apple Maps (MapKit):** Shows the map. It is also used for place search, address lookup and driving directions between a start and a destination. Apple receives the map area you are viewing, your search text and the coordinates being looked up or routed.
- **OSRM (router.project-osrm.org):** Builds driving routes for trips with stops, and is the backup when Apple Maps cannot build a route. It receives the coordinates of your start, stops and destination.
- **Nominatim (OpenStreetMap):** Backup place search and address lookup when Apple Maps search is unavailable. It receives your search text or the coordinates being looked up.
- **Open-Meteo:** Hourly weather forecasts along your route. It receives the coordinates of points along the route (sampled roughly every 40 miles) and the forecast range.
- **National Weather Service (api.weather.gov):** Active weather alerts in the United States. It receives the coordinates of points along your route, or of a point you select.
- **RainViewer:** The radar overlay. The app requests the list of current radar images and the radar map tiles for the area you are viewing.
- **Apple:** iOS platform services and App Store distribution.

Each provider has its own privacy practices and terms.

## Data storage and retention

- Saved routes are stored locally on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
- We do not require account creation to use the app.
- We do not operate servers that store your data. Third-party providers may keep request logs according to their own policies.
- We do not sell personal information.

## Data sharing

We share data only as needed to operate app features through the third-party services listed above, comply with legal obligations, or protect users and the app.

## Children's privacy

WeatherOnTheRoute is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## Your choices

- You can remove saved routes in the app.
- You can uninstall the app to remove local app data.
- You can manage location permissions in iOS Settings.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material updates will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
