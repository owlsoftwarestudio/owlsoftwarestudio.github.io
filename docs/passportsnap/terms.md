## What PassportSnap does

PassportSnap helps you take or choose a photo, checks it against the photo requirements for the document you select, replaces or cleans up the background where the rules allow it, and creates a digital photo and a 4×6 in print sheet. All face detection, background processing and checks run on your device. See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy) for details.

## No guarantee of acceptance

PassportSnap compares your photo with requirements based on published government photo specifications, such as size, head height, eye position, background and lighting. **We do not guarantee that any passport, visa or other office, agency or authority will accept a photo made with PassportSnap.**

- The final decision on any photo is made by the authority that receives it. Officials may reject a photo for reasons the app cannot detect.
- Photo rules change. The specifications in the app were checked on the date shown in the app, and may not reflect later changes.
- Some measurements in the app are estimates. Where a government does not publish a figure the app needs, such as exact head height or eye-line position, we derived it from the published rules and examples. These derived values currently apply to some documents, including the UK passport, Schengen visa, Canada passport and India passport, and may not match how the authority measures.
- Some documents are offered for practice only. For example, Canada requires passport photos from a commercial photographer, so PassportSnap does not export photos for that document.
- Automatic checks, cropping and background changes can make mistakes. Review every photo yourself before you use it.

**Before you apply, check the official photo rules for your document.** The app links to the official source for each document it supports. You are responsible for making sure your photo meets the current requirements, and for printing it correctly (at 100% size, with no cropping or borders added by the printer).
