# Privacy Policy for PassportSnap

**Effective date:** TBD (draft of October 31, 2026)

PassportSnap ("PassportSnap", "the app") is an iPhone app from Owl Software Studio ("we", "our", "us") that helps you take and prepare passport and visa photos. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how.

## Summary

- Your photos are processed on your iPhone. PassportSnap does not upload your photos to us or to any server.
- There is no account, no analytics, no tracking, no advertising and no third-party SDKs.
- We do not collect, receive or sell your personal information.

## Information the app processes

- **Photos you take or choose:** You can take a photo with the camera or pick one from your photo library with the iOS photo picker. PassportSnap uses Apple's Vision framework on your device to find your face and facial landmarks, separate you from the background, check things like centring, head size, eyes, lighting and background, and crop and resize the photo to the document you chose. All of this happens on your iPhone. Your photo, and any information derived from it such as face position or landmarks, is not sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Settings stored on your device:** The app saves a few preferences in its own storage on your iPhone (iOS UserDefaults): your default print layout, default camera, whether haptics are on, the documents you used recently and whether you have finished the welcome screens. This data stays on your device and we cannot see it.
- **Temporary export files:** When you share a photo or print sheet, the app writes a temporary copy to its own temporary folder on your iPhone so the share sheet can use it. The app deletes earlier copies the next time you share, and iOS may clear this folder at any time.

PassportSnap does not use face data for identification, does not build a profile of you, and does not keep a library of your photos.

## Permissions

- **Camera:** Used only to take your passport or visa photo.
- **Add to Photos:** Used only when you choose to save a finished photo or print sheet to your photo library.
- **Photo picker:** When you choose a photo, iOS gives the app only the photo you selected. The app does not ask for access to your whole library.

You can change camera and Photos permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

## Saving and sharing

Finished photos and print sheets leave the app only when you choose to save them to Photos or share them with the iOS share sheet (for example to Files, Mail, Messages, a print service or another app). They go only where you send them, and that destination's own privacy practices apply.

## In-app purchase

PassportSnap offers a one-time in-app purchase to unlock full features. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using Apple's StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment details, name or Apple ID. The app checks with the App Store whether your Apple ID has bought the unlock, and Apple handles that request under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Network use

PassportSnap does not send your data to any server we run. The only connections the app makes are:

- **App Store requests** handled by Apple's StoreKit, to show the price, make a purchase and restore or check your purchase.
- **Links you tap,** such as the official government page for a document, this policy or the Terms of Use, which open outside the app in your browser.
- **Contact support,** which opens your email app with a draft that includes the app version, device model and iOS version. Nothing is sent unless you send that email yourself.

## Third-party services

PassportSnap does not include third-party analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. The app uses Apple's iOS frameworks, such as Vision, AVFoundation, PhotosUI, Photos and StoreKit, which run on your device or are operated by Apple.

## Data retention and deletion

- We do not hold any of your data, so there is nothing for us to delete.
- Settings and temporary files stay on your iPhone until you delete the app, which removes them.
- Photos you saved to Photos or shared stay where you put them and are managed by you.

## Your rights by region

Depending on where you live, privacy laws give you rights over your personal data. These include the right to access, correct or erase your data and to object to or restrict its use under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR, the right to know, delete and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information under the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA (CCPA/CPRA), and similar rights under other laws, such as those in other U.S. states, Canada, Brazil, Australia and India.

PassportSnap does not collect personal data or send it off your device, so we hold no personal data about you to access, correct, delete or transfer, and we do not sell or share personal information. The data the app uses stays on your iPhone, and you can remove it at any time by deleting the app or the photos you saved.

Purchase data, such as your Apple ID and payment details, is handled by Apple under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). To exercise your rights over that data, contact Apple.

If you have questions about your rights or this policy, contact us at [support@owlsoftware.studio](mailto:support@owlsoftware.studio). You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

## Children's privacy

PassportSnap is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The app does not collect personal information from anyone. A parent or guardian can use PassportSnap to make a photo for a child; that photo is processed on the device in the same way and is not sent to us.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the app gains new features. When we do, we will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date above. If a change affects how your information is handled, we will update this page before that change takes effect.
